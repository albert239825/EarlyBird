## Core_summary
On January 10, 2026, at Bowdoin's Lubin Family Squash Center, the Tufts University women's squash team defeated host **Bowdoin College 8-1**, securing Tufts' third straight win while Bowdoin fell to 3-3 overall (2-2 home, 1-0 away, 1-1 NESCAC).[1][9] Tufts won 8 of 9 matches, with key victories including senior **Vharsha Dinesh** (No. 1, 11-7, 11-5, 11-3 over Darcy Weber, ending her 3-match skid), junior **Maria Arseniev** (No. 2, 11-2, 11-6, 4-11, 11-6 over Eshika Tangri), sophomore **Anika Goyal** (No. 3, 11-4, 11-6, 11-8 over Kira Gwadry, her 3rd straight), junior **Chloe Bergam** (No. 4, 11-8, 11-9, 11-7 over Linley Grosman, 3rd straight), first-year **Sejal Virk** (No. 5, 9-11, 11-4, 11-7, 2-11, 11-8 over Clare Collins), and sophomore **Indira Moshi** (No. 6, 13-15, 7-11, 11-4, 11-6, 11-5 over Amelie Matuch in ~1-hour match); Bowdoin won only No. 7 (details unspecified).[1]

**Bowdoin context**: Ranked No. 18 pre-match, they swept Wesleyan 9-0 on Jan. 7 (improving to 2-3) but had prior 9-0 losses to No. 5 Trinity, No. 12 Dartmouth (Dec. 8, Dartmouth won all 3-0 games, losing just 94 points total), and Connecticut (Jan. 11, 9-0); captains include Mira Karande '26 and Eshika Tangri '27, building on 2024-25's 12-6 record and top-20 finish with young talent like Elizabeth Walsh '26.[3][4][5][6][8][9] No expert opinions or statistical projections available; next for Bowdoin is vs. Hamilton (Jan. 17).[7] Similar 8-1 results occurred elsewhere (e.g., No. 19 Wesleyan over No. 23 Hamilton).[2]
