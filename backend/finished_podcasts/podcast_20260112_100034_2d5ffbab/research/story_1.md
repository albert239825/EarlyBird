## Core_summary
**Pope Leo XIV's meeting with Venezuelan opposition leader María Corina Machado**, Nobel Peace Prize 2025 winner, underscores Vatican engagement with Venezuela's pro-democracy struggle amid ongoing authoritarianism under Nicolás Maduro.[1][2][3] Machado, 58-year-old industrial engineer and Vente Venezuela coordinator, won opposition primary with >90% in 2023 but was barred from 2024 presidential ballot; she endorsed Edmundo González, whose opposition-documented victory (verified by independent experts) was rejected by Maduro, triggering crackdown and her hiding since August 2024.[1][3][5][6] Experts (Nobel Committee) praise her non-violent unification of divided opposition against Chavismo since 2010 National Assembly election (record votes), expulsion (2014), and founding Súmate (2002, election monitoring) and Atenea (1992, child aid).[2][3][4] Stats: Venezuela's economic collapse (plummeting oil, mismanagement, corruption, US sanctions) fueled 2024 protests; opposition tallies showed González landslide win.[6] Implications: Meeting signals potential papal mediation for "just, peaceful transition to democracy," amid repression, humanitarian crisis, and Maduro's grip since 2013; Machado's persistence (Magnitsky 2024 award) highlights risks but sustained global recognition.[3][5] No Maduro perspective in results; event lacks further 2026 details.[1-7]
